How to Use the English ⇄ Spanish Translator

Select your preferred direction using the tabs at the top (English → Spanish or Spanish → English), or click the swap button (⇄) anytime. Type or paste your text in the first box and click Translate Now for instant results.

Top 5 Common English & Spanish Phrases

Useful everyday phrases for travelers, students, and professionals:

  • Hello, how are you? — Hola, ¿cómo estás?
  • Thank you very much — Muchas gracias
  • How much does this cost? — ¿Cuánto cuesta esto?
  • I don't understand — No entiendo
  • Please — Por favor

Why Two-Way Translation Matters

Whether you are communicating with Spanish-speaking clients, learning a new language, or traveling abroad, having a fast two-way English and Spanish translator helps you understand and respond accurately without delays.
